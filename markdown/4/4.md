## What is jailbreaking?

The jailbreaking of LLM system is to force the system to generate harmful or unethical content. The most impactful example  scenario - Jailbreaking LLM's enables adversaries to automate spreading disinformation on social media. This is not the best advertisement for the creators of those systems.
